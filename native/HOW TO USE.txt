AC8 Mod Control 1.2.0 Experimental 5
Requires UE4SS Experimental v3.0.1-1152-ge3ba1016.

Close the game. Select ALL mods you want enabled. Enable MRP credits, then choose Custom amount (1 to 999,999,999) or Unlimited (999,999,999). Click Apply Selected Mods, which backs up the save and installs your selections. Unchecked mods are disabled.

The amount is a target balance, not an increment. Higher existing balances remain. Unlimited is a large one-time balance, not an infinite/spending freeze. Credits wait for a stable campaign hangar. F6 reapplies the selected target; reopen the tree to refresh the display. Let the game save normally.

Aircraft Tree is a separate option and does not enable Mission Access or grant parts purchases. Custom MRP was tested in game. The author confirmed Aircraft Tree purchases work and remain after saving. Not every node or campaign stage has been tested. Saved unlocks are not reversed by disabling mods.

Apply once, then play: the launcher can be closed after installation. Keep UE4SS and the installed scripts in place. F10 opens the FOV overlay during unpaused flight. Reapply to update or change options or the MRP target. Keep the launcher outside the game folder if desired.
For credits, allow about 10-15 seconds in the ready campaign hangar, then reopen the tree. For aircraft/loadouts, wait about 5-10 seconds; F7 is the manual fallback.
No scan clearance is claimed for this binary. Offline/single-player only.
